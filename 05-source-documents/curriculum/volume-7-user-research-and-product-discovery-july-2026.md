# 1. User Research

**Coverage basis:** 5 courses in the supplied pathway. **Practice lens:** research discipline.

## Capability and decision

User Research is uncertainty reduction. The skill is complete only when it changes a decision and leaves an inspectable trail from evidence to interpretation to action. Begin with the affected user or economic unit, the consequence of error, the available evidence, and the reversible decision that can be made now.

## Core concepts

- **Research Question:** Define it operationally, state what evidence establishes it, name its owner, and describe the decision it changes.
- **Method Fit:** Define it operationally, state what evidence establishes it, name its owner, and describe the decision it changes.
- **Sampling:** Define it operationally, state what evidence establishes it, name its owner, and describe the decision it changes.
- **Ethics:** Define it operationally, state what evidence establishes it, name its owner, and describe the decision it changes.
- **Evidence Quality:** Define it operationally, state what evidence establishes it, name its owner, and describe the decision it changes.

## Method

1. Write the decision, riskiest assumption, scope, population, timeframe, and exclusions.
2. Select a method whose evidence can answer the question; document why cheaper alternatives are insufficient.
3. Define the artifact, success criterion, guardrails, and stopping rule before observing results.
4. Execute with provenance, consent or permissions, accessibility, and contradictory evidence preserved.
5. Validate data quality, sampling, identity, measurement, and alternative explanations.
6. Compare at least two options and state trade-offs, residual risk, and operational burden.
7. Recommend a bounded next step with owner, rollout, rollback, and review date.

Findings are labeled as observation, inference, or causal evidence. The recommendation begins with a reversible cohort and identifies the evidence that would stop expansion.

## Measurement and diagnostics

Track **decision impact, evidence confidence, segment coverage, learning velocity**. Define the unit, denominator, observation or attribution window, data source, owner, and refresh cadence. Report the distribution and relevant segments instead of relying on a blended average.

## Failure modes and controls
